This laboratory focuses on summary fidelity, numerical verification, report scaffolding, and responsible use of Generative AI in workplace writing. The main objective is to understand how AI-generated summaries can be checked against an original source to ensure that important facts, figures, limitations, and meanings are accurately preserved.

The lab begins with a claim-by-claim fidelity check. The source contains 120 passenger comments, including 45 comments about long security queues, 30 about unclear directional signs, 20 about baggage delays, 15 about limited seating, and 10 positive comments about staff helpfulness. It also records the introduction of two temporary information desks for a two-week trial. Claims such as the trial being successful, passenger satisfaction declining, or financial impact being calculated are identified as unsupported and are removed.
A separate numerical-information check demonstrates that every important figure should match the original source. The lab emphasizes that numbers should not be changed, unnecessarily rounded, or invented.
The lab also identifies omissions and inventions. Missing information such as the total number of comments, positive staff feedback, and trial duration should be restored, while unsupported claims about success, financial impact, satisfaction percentages, or comparisons with other airports should be removed.
A corrected management summary and structured report scaffold are then developed. The scaffold separates factual information from report structure and uses **[VERIFY] placeholders** where evidence is unavailable. This helps prevent assumptions from being presented as confirmed facts.
Overall, LAB 8 develops skills in accurate summarisation, fact-checking, numerical verification, report organisation, peer review, and responsible AI-assisted workplace writing. It reinforces that AI summaries must be reviewed against the original source before being used for management or business decisions.
